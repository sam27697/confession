VERDICT: DROP.

The frame cost is small and the animation is smooth. Two things decide against
it anyway. /sent carried no client component at all, and this turns it into a
hydrating client route to carry a decoration; the app has held a five-component
client whitelist since week 11 and nothing here is worth the sixth. And the loop
runs continuously on the one screen whose entire message is that there is
nothing here yet, which is the screen most likely to be left open on a phone in
a pocket.

T05's own DROP criterion was "if the pure CSS radial vignette (B3-T03) achieves
equivalent atmospheric depth with zero CPU cycles and zero runtime JS". It does:
B3-T03 ships that vignette, it costs 0.0022 ms per frame of script (which is the
page, not the effect), and it adds no bytes to any route.
