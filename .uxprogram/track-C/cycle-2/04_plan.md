# Plan C2: Cognitive Ergonomics, Keystroke Elimination, and Form Comfort

## Ideation techniques used
1. **Physical Affordance & Tactile Mechanical Inversion:** Inverting rigid digital text inputs into elastic physical containers that dynamically expand with content weight (field-sizing: content), eliminating artificial box constraints.
2. **Conversation Partner Scaffolding:** Treating the anonymous confession prompt as an empathetic conversation partner offering structured conversational starters rather than a vacant form asking for input.
